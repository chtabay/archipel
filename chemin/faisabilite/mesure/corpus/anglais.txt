### mail | 12:41 | Gmail (pro) | User interviews - key insights (round 3)
Hi team,

Quick summary of this morning's interviews (5 participants):
- Most people order groceries on their phone while cooking, one hand busy, so the big buttons really matter.
- Two participants use the app on the train during their commute and lose connection in the tunnels, we need offline drafts.
- One older participant asked her granddaughter for help to find the promo code field. We should make it visible.
- Everyone loved the photos of the fresh vegetables and the bakery section, nobody understood the "smart basket" label.

Full notes and recordings are in the research folder: https://drive.example/research/round-3
Let's discuss at Thursday's crit.
